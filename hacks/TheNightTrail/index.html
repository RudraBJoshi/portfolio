THE NIGHT TRAIL

Mirpur Khas, August 1947. Leave before the roads close for good.

A short, fictionalized journey inspired by the 1947 Partition of India and Veera Hiranandani's novel The Night Diary, following the doctor Suresh, his mother Dadi, and his twin children Amil and Nisha as they cross the Thar Desert by night toward a new home across the border.

Before You Set Out

Dr. Suresh, his mother Dadi, and his twin children Amil and Nisha must leave Mirpur Khas together. The whole family travels either way, choose whose eyes you'll see the journey through.
